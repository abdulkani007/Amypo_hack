# Student Affairs, Campus Life & Grievance Redressal - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Directorate of Student Affairs
The Directorate of Student Affairs at Amypo Institute of Technology oversees extracurricular life, student clubs, collegiate counseling, health wellness, anti-ragging compliance, and student grievance redressal.

## Professional Student Chapters & Technical Clubs
Amypo hosts vibrant student chapters affiliated with international and national engineering societies:
- **IEEE Student Branch (STB6214)**: Organizes annual flagship conference, robotics symposia, and tech paper tracks.
- **ACM Student Chapter**: Conducts 24-hour hackathons, competitive programming leagues, and web development clinics.
- **Amypo Cyber Forensics Club (SOC Team)**: Hands-on capture-the-flag (CTF) challenges, network defense workshops, and vulnerability research.
- **SAE Collegiate Club**: Formula Bharat electric race car design, fabrication, and national track competitions.
- **Amypo Rotaract & NSS Wing**: Community social outreach, beach cleanups along ECR, blood donation camps, and rural school teaching.

## Campus Healthcare & Medical Support
- **Campus Health Centre**: Located in the Student Activity Center, Ground Floor. Open 24/7 with a resident medical doctor and two nursing officers.
- **Emergency Ambulance**: On-campus fully equipped ambulance on standby 24/7 (`+91-44-2834-0199`).
- **Student Counseling & Mental Wellness Desk**: Free confidential psychological counseling available Monday through Saturday.

## Anti-Ragging Committee & Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)
Amypo maintains a strict zero-tolerance code against ragging, harassment, or gender discrimination:
- **National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline**: `1800-180-5522`
- **Institutional Anti-Ragging Hotline**: `+91-44-2834-0190`
- **ICC Presiding Officer**: Dr. R. Nalini, Professor of Computer Applications (`icc@amypo.edu.in`)

## Student Grievance Redressal Cell (SGRC)
Students experiencing any grievance concerning examinations, faculty evaluations, hostel facilities, or administrative billing can register a formal grievance through the Amypo Student ERP grievance module (`https://erp.amypo.edu.in/grievance`). The SGRC committee meets every alternate Wednesday to resolve registered complaints.
