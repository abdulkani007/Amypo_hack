# Campus Placement Policy & Recruitment Guidelines - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Corporate Placement Mission
The Department of Corporate Relations and Placements at Amypo Institute of Technology facilitates campus placements, career counseling, technical pre-placement training, and industry interface sessions for all engineering graduates.

## Placement Eligibility Criteria
1. Students become eligible for on-campus and virtual recruitment drives commencing from the **7th Semester**.
2. **Academic Standing**: Minimum cumulative grade point average of **6.0 CGPA** across all completed semesters.
3. **Backlog Rules**: Candidate must have **zero standing / active arrears** at the time of company registration. Some marquee recruiters (Tier-1 companies) mandate a higher CGPA of 7.5+ and no history of past arrears.
4. **Training Attendance**: Minimum 85% attendance in mandatory pre-placement soft skills, competitive coding, and aptitude bootcamps conducted by the Placement Cell.

## Placement Policy Rules ("One Student, One Job")
- **Standard Offer Tier**: Once a student secures an offer with a compensation package up to ₹7.0 Lakhs Per Annum (LPA), they are considered placed and removed from subsequent standard drives.
- **Dream Offer Upgrade**: A placed student may participate in Dream Company recruitment drives offering a salary package of **₹12.0 LPA or higher** (Tier-1 Super Dream).
- **Offer Acceptance**: Once a student accepts a corporate letter of intent (LOI), they are obligated to join the company upon graduation, unless they pursue verified higher studies.

## Key Recruiters & Salary Statistics
- **Top Marquee Recruiters**: Zoho Corporation, Tata Consultancy Services (TCS Digital & Ninja), Infosys, Cognizant, L&T Technology Services, Cisco Systems, Qualcomm, Bosch Global Software, Hexaware.
- **Average Compensation Package**: ₹6.8 LPA
- **Highest International / National Package**: ₹38.5 LPA (Offered by Global Cloud Security Firm)

## 🚨 MANDATORY SECURITY WARNING: RECRUITMENT SCAMS & UNAUTHORIZED DRIVES
> **CRITICAL RECRUITMENT FRAUD NOTICE**:
> 1. All official Amypo recruitment drives, shortlists, schedules, and interview links are communicated **EXCLUSIVELY through the Amypo Placement ERP Portal** and official institutional emails ending with `@amypo.edu.in`.
> 2. **NO RECRUITER CHARGES MONEY**: Neither Amypo Institute of Technology nor any genuine corporate employer will ever ask a student to pay an "interview processing fee", "background check charge", "uniform fee", or "joining security deposit".
> 3. If you receive an unsolicited SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram message stating you have been selected for TCS, Zoho, or Amazon through Amypo and need to transfer money to confirm your seat, **IT IS 100% A PHISHING SCAM**.
