# Scholarship Schemes & Financial Assistance - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Overview of Financial Aid
Amypo Institute of Technology believes that financial constraints should never obstruct academic excellence. The institute facilitates both institutional merit scholarships and Tamil Nadu / Central Government welfare scholarship schemes.

## Institutional Scholarship Schemes
1. **Amypo Academic Excellence Merit Scholarship**:
   - **Eligibility**: Awarded to the top 5% of students in every academic branch with an annual CGPA of **9.0 or higher** and zero backlogs.
   - **Financial Benefit**: **50% concession on tuition fees** for the subsequent academic year.
   - **Application**: Automatically evaluated by the Office of the Controller of Examinations and Academic Registrar; no separate application is required.
2. **Sports & Athletic Honors Scholarship**:
   - **Eligibility**: Students who represent the institution or state in All India Inter-University (AIU) tournaments, Khelo India, or national athletic meets and secure podium positions.
   - **Financial Benefit**: **100% hostel and mess fee waiver** + **50% tuition fee waiver**.
3. **Economic Hardship Support Grant**:
   - **Eligibility**: Students whose cumulative parental annual income is below **₹2,00,000** with a sustained minimum CGPA of 7.5.
   - **Financial Benefit**: One-time grant of **₹35,000 per academic year** credited against tuition charges.

## Government & State Welfare Schemes
1. **Tamil Nadu Government First Graduate Tuition Concession**:
   - **Eligibility**: Students admitted through single-window TNEA counseling who are the first in their immediate family to pursue a university degree.
   - **Benefit**: **₹25,000 per annum** direct fee reduction sanctioned by the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE).
   - **Required Credentials**: First Graduate Certificate and Joint Declaration signed by parent and candidate, attested by the jurisdictional Tahsildar.
2. **Post-Matric Scholarship for SC / ST / SCA Communities**:
   - **Eligibility**: SC/ST/SCA students with annual parental income below ₹2,50,000.
   - **Benefit**: Complete tuition fee reimbursement and compulsory non-refundable charges credited via National Scholarship Portal (NSP) / State Portal.
3. **BC / MBC / DNC Welfare Scholarship**:
   - Provided by the Department of Backward Classes and Minorities Welfare for hostellers and day scholars.
