# Attendance Regulations & Policy - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Academic Attendance Standard
Under the Autonomous Academic Regulations of Amypo Institute of Technology, regular classroom engagement is essential for academic continuity and practical competence. Attendance is recorded on a per-period basis using RFID biometric turnstiles and smart digital classroom registers.

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
Every student is mandated to maintain a **minimum cumulative attendance of 75.0%** across all theory and laboratory courses registered in a semester to become eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations.

## Condonation of Attendance Shortage (65.0% to 74.99%)
Students having cumulative attendance between **65.0% and 74.99%** may apply for attendance condonation subject to the following stringent conditions:
1. **Permissible Reasons**:
   - Hospitalization or serious medical illness.
   - Representing the institution in state/national sports, AIU cultural competitions, or official hackathons.
   - Authorized participation in official NCC/NSS training camps.
2. **Medical Proof Submission Procedure**:
   - The student must submit an official written application along with an original Medical Fitness Certificate signed and stamped by a Registered Medical Practitioner (MBBS or higher).
   - The medical certificate and hospital discharge summary must be submitted to the respective Head of Department (HOD) and the Office of the Dean (Academics) **within 7 working days** of returning to the campus. Medical certificates submitted at the end of the semester will not be accepted.
3. **Condonation Processing Fee**:
   - A non-refundable processing fee of **₹1,500** must be remitted via the Amypo Student ERP portal upon preliminary approval by the Academic Review Board.

## Detention Regulations (Below 65.0%)
- **Strict Detention Rule**: Any student with less than **65.0% cumulative attendance** is automatically marked as **DETAINED** for that semester.
- **Consequences of Detention**:
  - The student is strictly barred from appearing in the End-Semester Examinations for that semester.
  - No condonation or exemption can be granted under any circumstances, including medical reasons.
  - The student must re-register and redo all coursework and laboratories of that semester when offered in the subsequent academic year.

## Real-Time Attendance Monitoring
Students and parents can inspect live attendance metrics at any time through the Amypo Student ERP mobile application or web dashboard (`https://erp.amypo.edu.in`). Automated alerts are dispatched to parents via SMS when a student's attendance drops below 80%.
