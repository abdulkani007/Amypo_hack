# Amypo Institute of Technology (AIT Chennai) - College Overview

## Institutional Profile
Amypo Institute of Technology is a premier autonomous engineering and technology institution located on the Rajiv Gandhi Salai (OMR IT Expressway) in Semmancheri, Chennai, Tamil Nadu (PIN: 600119). Established in 2008, the institute is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, affiliated with the State Technological University, and accredited with an 'A++' Grade by NAAC.

## Mission and Vision
The institute's mission is to foster world-class technical education, research in autonomous computing, cyber defense, and sustainable engineering, producing ethical leaders equipped to solve complex socio-technical challenges.

## Campus Architecture & Location
- **Main Campus Address**: No. 42, Rajiv Gandhi Salai, Semmancheri, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600119.
- **Landmark**: Near Sholinganallur Junction, directly opposite the Cyber Hub IT Park.
- **Campus Span**: 48 acres of Wi-Fi-enabled smart green campus with rainwater harvesting, solar microgrids, and electric vehicle charging bays.
