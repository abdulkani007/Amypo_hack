# Internship Guidelines & Fraud Warning Policy - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Institutional Internship Regulations
Internships provide valuable experiential learning for undergraduate engineering students. The Centre for Industry Relations (CIR) and the Corporate Placement Cell oversee all summer, winter, and final-semester industrial internships.

## Student Eligibility Criteria
1. Students become eligible for external industrial internships from the **5th Semester onwards**.
2. Must possess a minimum cumulative grade point average (**CGPA >= 6.5**).
3. Must have zero standing active backlogs/arrears at the time of application.
4. Final semester (8th Semester) Capstone Industry Internships require prior approval from the Head of Department (HOD) and Dean (Academics).

## Internship Verification & Approval Workflow
Before commencing any internship, students must complete the following formal protocol:
1. **Offer Letter Ingestion**: Submit the formal corporate offer letter to the Department Internship Coordinator.
2. **NOC Generation**: The department validates the employer's credibility and issues a **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** endorsed by the HOD and Dean of Industry Relations.
3. **Weekly Progress Log**: Students must upload weekly progress summaries and attendance signatures on the Amypo CIR portal.
4. **Final Evaluation**: A 2-credit internship evaluation requires an authentic completion certificate and an industrial mentor sign-off.

## 🚨 CRITICAL SECURITY & SCAM ADVISORY ON FAKE INTERNSHIP OFFERS
The Corporate Placement Cell and Institutional Cyber Security Desk issue the following strict advisory regarding fraudulent internship recruitment campaigns targeting college students:

### Golden Rule of Legitimacy
> **OFFICIAL AMYPO INSTITUTIONAL RULE**:
> **A GENUINE CORPORATE INTERNSHIP NEVER DEMANDS MONEY FROM A CANDIDATE.**
> No legitimate company, tech firm, research institute, or startup affiliated with Amypo Institute of Technology will ever ask you to pay a "registration fee", "security deposit", "documentation fee", "training charge", or "laptop delivery deposit".

### Red Flags & Scam Indicators
If you receive an internship offer via email, SMS, LinkedIn, Telegram, or WhatsApp displaying any of the following characteristics, treat it as a **POTENTIAL SCAM**:
1. **Demand for Upfront Payment**: The recruiter requests ₹1,500 - ₹5,000 via Google Pay, PhonePe, or UPI for "registration", "ID card generation", or "training kits".
2. **Generic Sender Domains**: The offer originates from `@gmail.com`, `@yahoo.com`, or `@outlook.com` instead of a verified corporate domain (e.g., `@tcs.com`, `@zoho.com`).
3. **Instant Selection Without Assessment**: The candidate is offered an internship within minutes of applying without any formal technical interview or coding assessment.
4. **Excessive / Unrealistic Stipend**: Offering abnormally high stipends (e.g., ₹50,000/month for 1st/2nd-year basic data entry or web design).
5. **Urgency & Coercive Language**: Threats such as "Slot expires in 2 hours if payment is not confirmed" or "Legal penalty will be issued if you refuse the offer".
6. **Payment QR Codes Sent on WhatsApp**: Requesting scans of unverified merchant QR codes.

### Verification Protocol for Students
If you encounter a suspicious internship communication claiming to be associated with or approved by Amypo Institute:
- **DO NOT SEND ANY MONEY.**
- **DO NOT CLICK ON UNVERIFIED WEBLINKS OR DOWNLOAD ZIP/APK ATTACHMENTS.**
- Forward the email or message to the Corporate Placement Cell at `placement@amypo.edu.in` or visit the Placement Office (Block B, Ground Floor, Room 102).
- You can also run the message through the campus **Security Verification Console** in the Amypo Digital Reception portal.
