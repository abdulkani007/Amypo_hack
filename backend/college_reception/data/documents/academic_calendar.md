# Academic Calendar & Key Operational Dates - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Academic Year 2026 - 2027 Schedule

### Odd Semester (Semesters III, V, VII)
- **Commencement of Class Work**: July 15, 2026
- **First Internal Continuous Assessment Test (CAT-1)**: August 24 - 29, 2026
- **Mid-Semester Academic Review & Parent-Teacher Meeting**: September 12, 2026
- **Second Internal Continuous Assessment Test (CAT-2)**: October 12 - 17, 2026
- **Annual Inter-Collegiate Cultural Festival ("TARANG 2026")**: October 23 - 24, 2026
- **Last Working Day / Attendance Finalization**: November 06, 2026
- **Practical & Project Viva-Voce Examinations**: November 10 - 18, 2026
- **Commencement of End-Semester Theory Examinations**: November 23, 2026
- **Winter Vacation & Industry Internship Break**: December 21, 2026 - January 10, 2027

### Even Semester (Semesters IV, VI, VIII)
- **Commencement of Class Work**: January 11, 2027
- **First Internal Continuous Assessment Test (CAT-1)**: February 15 - 20, 2027
- **National Technical Symposium & Hackathon ("AMYPO HACKS")**: March 05 - 06, 2027
- **Second Internal Continuous Assessment Test (CAT-2)**: March 29 - April 03, 2027
- **Last Working Day for Coursework**: April 24, 2027
- **Practical / Final Capstone Viva Examinations**: April 28 - May 05, 2027
- **End-Semester Theory Examinations**: May 10 - May 29, 2027
- **Annual Graduation Ceremony & Convocation**: June 26, 2027
- **Summer Vacation & Faculty Research Sabbatical**: June 01 - July 12, 2027

## Office Working Hours & Holiday Guidelines
- Standard college office hours run Monday through Friday from 08:30 AM to 05:00 PM.
- First and Third Saturdays are full academic instructional days following a Wednesday timetable.
- Second and Fourth Saturdays, all Sundays, and Tamil Nadu Government Gazetted Holidays are declared institutional holidays.
- Real-time calendar notifications and adjustments are synchronized to the Amypo Student ERP dashboard.
