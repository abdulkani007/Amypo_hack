# Examination Rules & Controller of Examinations - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Academic Examination Schedule
The academic year at Amypo Institute of Technology is divided into two examination cycles:
- **Odd Semester Examinations**: Conducted during November - December.
- **Even Semester Examinations**: Conducted during April - May.
- **Supplementary / Fast-Track Arrear Exams**: Conducted in July for eligible graduating students.

## Examination Hall Entry Rules & Protocols
1. **Reporting Timelines**:
   - Candidates must report outside their designated Examination Hall **30 minutes prior** to the scheduled commencement time (Morning session begins at 09:30 AM, Afternoon session begins at 02:00 PM).
   - Examination hall entry doors are strictly locked **10 minutes before** the start time. Late arrivals after commencement are not permitted entry under any circumstances.
2. **Mandatory Credentials Required**:
   - **Physical Amypo Student Photo ID Card** with readable barcode.
   - **Official Examination Hall Ticket** printed from the Amypo ERP portal, carrying the student's recent photograph and Chief Superintendent stamp.
   - Students without a physical ID card or hall ticket must obtain an Emergency Verification Pass from the Office of the Controller of Examinations (Room 204) before entering the hall.
3. **Prohibited Devices & Items**:
   - Mobile phones, smart watches, Bluetooth fitness trackers, earphones, and digital communication devices (strictly barred from the hall premises; no storage facility is provided).
   - Programmable calculators, graphing devices, or scientific calculators with alphanumeric text memory (only non-programmable fx-82/fx-991 models are permitted).
   - Books, revision summaries, loose paper sheets, scribbling pads, or pencil cases with printed text.

## Examination Malpractice & Disciplinary Penalties
Any student found possessing unauthorized material, copying from others, exchanging calculators, writing on hall tickets, or communicating during the exam will face immediate booking under the Institutional Examination Malpractice Code:
- Immediate confiscation of answer script and hall ticket.
- Instant debarment from all remaining examination papers of the active semester.
- Mandatory summons before the Examination Malpractice Inquiry Committee.
- In severe cases (impersonation, digital communication), cancellation of the entire semester and suspension for up to one academic year.

## Revaluation & Script Photocopy Rules
- Students can apply for a digital photocopy of their evaluated answer script within 7 days of result declaration upon payment of ₹400 per paper.
- Revaluation applications must be submitted via the ERP portal within 10 days of results, accompanied by a fee of ₹750 per course.
