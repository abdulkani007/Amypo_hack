# Campus Infrastructure & Academic Facilities - Amypo Institute of Technology

## State-of-the-Art Smart Campus
Amypo Institute of Technology spans 48 landscaped acres on the IT Expressway (OMR) in Semmancheri, Chennai. The campus integrates sustainable green architecture, smart building automation, and high-performance computing centers.

## Central Library & Digital Knowledge Center
- **Collection**: Over 65,000 printed volumes, 12,000 standard engineering reference titles, and subscription to 180 national and international print journals.
- **Digital Library**: 120 high-speed multimedia terminal workstations offering uninterrupted access to IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Elsevier ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and NPTEL courseware.
- **Operating Hours**:
  - Regular Academic Days: 08:00 AM to 09:00 PM.
  - Examination Periods: 08:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
  - Sundays: 10:00 AM to 04:00 PM.

## High-Performance Computing & GPU Research Cluster
- **Location**: Block A, 4th Floor (Central Computing Facility)
- **Infrastructure**: Dedicated private cloud cluster comprising 16 NVIDIA A100 Tensor Core GPUs (80GB each), 1.2 Petabytes of NVMe flash storage, and 10 Gbps redundant optical fiber backbone.
- **Access Policy**: Open to faculty, research scholars, and undergraduate students working on approved capstone projects in AI, Deep Learning, and Cybersecurity Forensics.

## Sports Complex & Athletic Grounds
- **Outdoor Facilities**: Standard 400-meter 8-lane athletic track, full-sized cricket turf ground with floodlights, football ground, two synthetic tennis courts, and three basketball courts.
- **Indoor Sports Arena**: Multi-purpose air-conditioned sports pavilion featuring four wooden badminton courts, two table tennis arenas, and an international standard gymnasium with certified fitness trainers.

## Food Courts & Campus Dining
- **Central Food Court (The Oasis)**: Multi-cuisine hygienic dining facility operating from 07:30 AM to 09:30 PM offering South Indian, North Indian, fresh juice counters, and bakery snacks at subsidized student rates.
- **Coffee Kiosks**: Two Cafe Coffee Day and Bru outlets situated in Block A and the Central Library concourse.

## Campus Transport & Bus Routes
- A fleet of 42 air-conditioned and non-AC buses covers key transit routes across Greater Chennai, including Tambaram, Velachery, Adyar, Koyambedu, Central Station, Guindy, and Chengalpattu.
- Transport Desk: Administrative Block, Ground Floor, Room 102 (`transport@amypo.edu.in`).

## IT Helpdesk & Campus Wi-Fi Services
- Seamless 802.11ax Wi-Fi 6 connectivity across all classrooms, hostels, and open lawns with WPA3 enterprise security.
- **IT Support Helpline**: `ithelpdesk@amypo.edu.in` or Phone: `+91-44-2834-0105`.
