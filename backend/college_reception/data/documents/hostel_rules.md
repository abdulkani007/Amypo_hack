# Hostel Accommodation & Residence Guidelines - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Campus Residential Facilities
Amypo Institute of Technology provides secure, air-cooled, and fully equipped residential hostel complexes for undergraduate and postgraduate scholars within the guarded campus perimeters.

## Residential Blocks
1. **Kaveri Block (Boys Hostel)**: Accommodates 450 students. 2-sharing and 3-sharing air-conditioned and non-AC rooms with attached washrooms.
2. **Bhavani Block (Boys Hostel)**: Accommodates 380 students. 3-sharing and 4-sharing non-AC rooms with centralized modern washrooms and study rooms.
3. **Krishna Block (Girls Hostel)**: Accommodates 400 students. 2-sharing and 3-sharing air-conditioned and non-AC rooms with recreation lounges.
4. **Vaigai Block (Girls Hostel)**: Accommodates 320 students. 3-sharing non-AC rooms with private study balconies and round-the-clock female security wardens.

## Annual Residential Fee Schedule
- **Air-Conditioned 2-Sharing Room (with 4-meal daily catering)**: ₹95,000 per academic year.
- **Non-AC 3-Sharing Room (with 4-meal daily catering)**: ₹75,000 per academic year.
- **Refundable Hostel Caution Deposit**: ₹10,000 (one-time deposit during admission).
- **Mess Inclusions**: Healthy South Indian and North Indian vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus served across separate dining halls. Special festive meals every month.

## Hostel Code of Conduct & Curfew Rules
1. **Curfew & Biometric Check-in**:
   - **Night Curfew is strictly 08:30 PM**.
   - All resident students must enter their designated hostel block and scan their biometric RFID ID at the turnstiles prior to 08:30 PM.
   - Any late arrival without prior formal gate-pass approval will be recorded as a violation. Three unexcused late entries will result in parental notification and disciplinary review.
2. **Outing & Leave Protocols**:
   - **Day Outing**: Permitted on Saturdays and Sundays between 09:00 AM and 07:30 PM after obtaining an electronic gate pass generated via the Amypo Student ERP app.
   - **Home Leave**: Requires digital parental approval submitted via the parent portal 24 hours prior to travel.
3. **Strict Zero-Tolerance Anti-Ragging Policy**:
   - Ragging in any form is a cognizable criminal offense punishable under Tamil Nadu Prohibition of Ragging Act and Supreme Court mandates.
   - Offending students face immediate expulsion from the hostel, suspension from the institution, and lodging of an official police FIR.
4. **Prohibited Appliances**:
   - Electric kettles, heating rods, induction cooktops, iron boxes, and music amplifiers are strictly prohibited in hostel rooms.
   - Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, narcotics, or dangerous substances will result in immediate permanent expulsion.
