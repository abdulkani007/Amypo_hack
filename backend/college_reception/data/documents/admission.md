# Admission Procedures & Guidelines - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Undergraduate (B.Tech / B.E.) Admission Quotas
Admissions to undergraduate degree programs at Amypo Institute of Technology are conducted under two official admission streams:
1. **Government Single Window Counseling (TNEA Quota - 65% of seats)**: Administered through the Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA) single-window counseling system based on Higher Secondary (10+2) Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry aggregate scores.
2. **Consortium Merit Management Quota (35% of seats)**: Administered through the Amypo Institutional Merit Entrance and 10+2 normalized marks.

## Eligibility Criteria
- **Academic Qualification**: Candidates must have passed the Higher Secondary Examination (10+2) of the State Board or equivalent CBSE/ICSE board with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as core subjects.
- **Minimum Marks Requirement**:
  - General Category: Minimum aggregate of 50% in PCM.
  - BC / BCM Category: Minimum aggregate of 45% in PCM.
  - MBC / DNC Category: Minimum aggregate of 40% in PCM.
  - SC / SCA / ST Category: Passing grade in 10+2 with PCM subjects.
- **Lateral Entry (Direct 2nd Year)**: Candidates holding a 3-year Diploma in Engineering or B.Sc. degree with Mathematics with at least 50% aggregate (40% for reserved categories).

## Mandatory Documents Required for Verification
During physical document verification at the Admissions Office (Administrative Block, Room 101), candidates must produce the original and two sets of self-attested photocopies of the following credentials:
1. 10th Standard (SSLC) Statement of Marks / Date of Birth Certificate.
2. 11th Standard Mark Sheet.
3. 12th Standard (HSC) Permanent Mark Certificate.
4. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate from the institution last attended.
5. Community / Caste Certificate issued by competent revenue authority (Tahsildar level) for quota claims.
6. Nativity Certificate (if applicable for Tamil Nadu residency reservation).
7. First Graduate Certificate and Joint Declaration Form (for candidates claiming First Graduate fee concession).
8. Allotment Order issued by the TNEA Counseling Authority (for Government Quota).
9. Aadhaar Card copy (for identity verification and student ERP onboarding).
10. Five recent passport-sized color photographs with white background.

## Admissions Security & Fraud Advisory
Amypo Institute of Technology maintains a strictly merit-based admission protocol. The institution **does not appoint any external admission brokers, educational agents, or consultancies**. The college never requests cash handoffs, direct UPI transfers to personal telephone numbers, or donations for seat allocations. Report any unauthorized admission agent to `security@amypo.edu.in`.
