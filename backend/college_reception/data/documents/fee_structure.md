# Fee Structure & Payment Policies - Amypo Institute of Technology

## Academic Tuition Fee Structure (Per Semester)
Tuition fees at Amypo Institute of Technology are structured based on department laboratory requirements and computational resource allocation:

| Department | Semester Tuition Fee (INR) | Annual Tuition Fee (INR) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) | ₹85,000 | ₹1,70,000 |
| Information Technology (IT) | ₹85,000 | ₹1,70,000 |
| Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AIDS) | ₹85,000 | ₹1,70,000 |
| Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) | ₹75,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) | ₹75,000 | ₹1,50,000 |
| Mechanical Engineering (MECH) | ₹70,000 | ₹1,40,000 |

## Annual Institutional & Auxiliary Charges
In addition to semester tuition fees, all enrolled undergraduate students pay the following annual auxiliary charges billed at the commencement of each Odd Semester:
- **Central Library & Digital IEEE/ACM Access**: ₹3,000 per annum
- **Advanced Laboratory Development & Consumables Fee**: ₹6,000 per annum
- **Student Activities, Cultural, & Inter-Collegiate Sports Fund**: ₹2,500 per annum
- **Campus High-Speed Wi-Fi & Cloud Infrastructure**: ₹3,000 per annum
- **Group Medical & Accident Insurance Policy**: ₹1,500 per annum
- **One-Time Caution Deposit (100% Refundable upon graduation)**: ₹10,000 (billed during 1st Semester admission only)

## Examination Fees
Examination charges are levied per semester by the Office of the Controller of Examinations:
- Theory examination paper: ₹300 per subject
- Practical/Laboratory examination: ₹450 per subject
- Mini-Project / Final Year Capstone Project Viva-Voce: ₹800
- Official Grade Sheet & Transcript issuance: ₹200 per semester

## Approved Payment Methods
All fee payments must be remitted exclusively through the following official institutional gateways:
1. **Amypo Student ERP Portal**: Log in at `https://erp.amypo.edu.in/fees` using student credentials. Payments can be processed via NetBanking, Credit Card, or Debit Card through the integrated SBI/Canara Bank PG.
2. **Virtual Account NEFT/RTGS**: Each registered student is assigned a unique alphanumeric Canara Bank Virtual Account Number (e.g., `AITCSE2026xxxx`). Students must verify the exact account number within their ERP dashboard before initiating bank transfers.
3. **On-Campus Bank Counter**: Canara Bank Amypo Campus Branch (Challan counters open 10:00 AM - 03:30 PM).

## STRICT SECURITY WARNING ON FEE PAYMENT FRAUD
> **CRITICAL INSTITUTIONAL ADVISORY**:
> Amypo Institute of Technology **NEVER** authorizes fee collection through personal UPI IDs, WhatsApp QR code screenshots, or telephone payment requests. 
> Official fee receipts are generated automatically inside the student's ERP portal with a cryptographic digital signature and verification QR. 
> If you receive an SMS or email urging you to pay fees immediately to avoid exam debarment via an unverified link, **DO NOT PAY**. Immediately alert the Finance Desk at `accounts@amypo.edu.in` or the Exam Cell at `examcell@amypo.edu.in`.
